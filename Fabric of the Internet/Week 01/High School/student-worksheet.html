Reference

Key Vocabulary

  • InternetA decentralized global network of networks.
  • RouterForwards packets between networks, choosing a next hop for each one.
  • TopologyHow devices are arranged and connected to each other.
  • ScalabilityHow well a design holds up as it grows.
  • IP AddressA unique numeric identifier for a device on a network.
  • Next HopThe next router a packet is forwarded to on its way to a destination.
  • RedundancyMultiple paths so the network tolerates failures.
  • Single Point of FailureA component whose failure disconnects part of the network.
  • PacketA small numbered unit of data.
  • TCPGuarantees delivery and order — at the cost of speed.
  • UDPFast, with no delivery or order guarantee.

Tradeoffs

TCP vs UDP

The tradeoff
TCP = reliable + ordered, but slower (it confirms and resends).
UDP = fast, but no guarantees (packets may arrive out of order or not at all).
