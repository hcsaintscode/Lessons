Journal Prompt

  • We use the word "problem" for lots of different situations.
  • What are some common steps we can use to solve many different kinds of problems?
Write for 3 minutes.

The Problem Solving Process

01
Define
Figure out exactly what problem you are solving.
02
Prepare
Brainstorm ideas and make a plan.
03
Try
Put your plan into action.
04
Reflect
Look back and learn for next time.

Define

  • What problem are you trying to solve?
  • What are your constraints?
  • What does success look like?

Prepare

  • Brainstorm / research possible solutions
  • Compare pros and cons
  • Make a plan

Try

  • Put your plan into action.

Reflect

  • How do your results compare to your goals?
  • What can you learn or do better next time?
  • What new problems have you discovered?

It's a Cycle, Not a Line

Reflect often sends you right back to Define.
Define → Prepare → Try → Reflect ↻
Reflect ↻ back to Define
