Your Mission

Specify, build, iterate, and ship a real web app that solves a problem you care about. Prompting an AI builder is a form of SPECIFICATION — the clearer your spec, the better it builds. You'll plan on paper, build with lovable.dev, evaluate what the AI produces, and ship a deployed app.

DPTR IOSP Specify → Build → Evaluate → Ship
⚠ Credits lovable's free plan gives about 5 build messages per day, no rollover. Plan your full spec here first. One precise prompt beats ten vague ones. Out of credits = a spec / iteration-planning day.

Step 3 · Specify

Draft Your Build Prompt

Turn your IOSP spec into ONE structured prompt. Here's a strong example:

Example — Study-Session Planner Build a study-session planner web app. INPUTS: a form to add a subject, a due date, and estimated hours. OUTPUTS: a prioritized list sorted by due date, and total hours this week. STORAGE: persist the sessions. PROCESSING: sort by due date (comparing), flag anything due within 2 days (if/then), sum weekly hours (counting). Clean minimal UI for a high-school student; use simple local persistence.
