What Is a Computer?

Week 2 Student Worksheet · Problem Solving & Computing
Reference

Key Vocabulary

  • ComputerA machine that works with information.
  • InputThe information a computer gets.
  • OutputThe information a computer gives.
  • ProcessingThe thinking work — turning input into output.
  • StorageSaving information for later.

Four Kinds of Processing

  • If / ThenMaking a decision: if something is true, then do a certain thing.
  • ComparingLooking at two things to see how they are alike or different (bigger, smaller, equal).
  • Finding a MatchSearching through information to find something that fits.
  • CountingKeeping track of how many.
